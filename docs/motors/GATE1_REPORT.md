## 4. Gliding assays (decision E): none in the corpus

All 793 records are `single_molecule`. The first run labelled 3 records as gliding, but all three were single-molecule
force studies whose readout the pattern missed ("laser trap", "centrifuge microscope"); the pattern now recognises those.
**The SMASH-BIO harvest only collects force-spectroscopy papers**, so gliding-only papers are not in the corpus at all.
Including them needs a harvest change: motor-specific Europe PMC queries for gliding and motility assays, as the design
doc's "Pipeline changes" already proposes. **Question 1 below.**

## 6. Records for your review

`docs/motors/REVIEW_CLASSIFICATION.md`: the 20 least certain records per family. Certainty measures how unambiguously
the **family** resolved, not whether the paper is a **motor study** (e.g. an instrument-methods paper that mentions RSC
scores 1.00). Since A dropped the class requirement, the RNA polymerase, ribosome, helicase and SMC families now include
binding and structure studies, not only motility; the gold set's `is_processive_motor_study` field measures that rate.
