Simulation Beta

Pulling simulations, indexed separately

Simulated unfolding and unbinding live in their own section, with their own records (SIM-######), schema and downloads. A simulation is never listed as an experiment. Where the molecule matches, the two are linked and the match level says how close the comparison is.

Open simulations (beta)

Model, protocol and units

Each record names the model family, the pulling protocol and the unit system the source wrote. A reduced-unit value stays in reduced units unless the paper states a conversion.

Compared, not merged

Links to SMASH-BIO and MechanoProDB experiments carry a match level. A homolog link is a molecule-name token only, and the two sets of numbers are shown side by side.
