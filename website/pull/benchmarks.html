Yellow: the first contacts to break. Blue → orange: residues losing their native contacts. Make your own from any result on the simulator.

Titin I27: one pathway (10/10 runs)

In every run the N-terminal residues 1–7 separate from residues 71–77 first, then from 78–84. The terminal strand pairs carry the load, and the core follows.

Ubiquitin N–C: two pathways

7 of 10 runs open at the β1–β5 pair (residues 1–6 ↔ 67–72) first. In 2 of 10, the β1/β2–α1 region (13–18 ↔ 25–30) gives way first; one run matched neither group.

GFP pulling directions (gate 2b)

Protocol P_std (0.5 Å/τ, 20 replicas). Simulated peak tension against the unfolding force Dietz et al. (2006) measured for each pair of attachment points. The model's ranking follows how much chain lies between the attachment points, the signature of drag.

Hairpin class benchmark (gate 4)

The one fully reported hairpin of Woodside et al. (PNAS 2006), 20R55/4T, has F½ = 13.2 ± 0.7 pN and Δx = 18 ± 2 nm at 23 °C, 200 mM KCl. Its exact sequence could not be retrieved, so the gate uses 30 random 20-bp stems with the same GC content (11 G/C) and loop.
