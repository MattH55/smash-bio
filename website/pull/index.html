Beta · runs in your browser

Pull a molecule

Load a protein structure or type a DNA/RNA sequence, choose where to attach the handles and how hard to pull, and get simulated force–extension curves, unfolding events and the order in which the structure comes apart. The engine is compiled to WebAssembly and runs on this computer; structures are fetched from RCSB, AlphaFold DB or UniProt and nothing you enter is uploaded. What the models can and cannot tell you.

1What to pull

Loads the AlphaFold DB model. Residues with pLDDT below the cut-off keep their chain but lose their native contacts.

Exact-sequence match against UniProt (by CRC64 checksum, so only the checksum is sent), then the AlphaFold DB model of the best hit. A sequence with no exact match cannot be simulated here: structure prediction does not run in a browser.
