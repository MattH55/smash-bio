How modules are defined

Modules follow Halma 2025 (Table 3), Haghizadeh, Iftikhar, Dandpat and Simpson 2023 (biological questions) and Malinowska, van Mameren, Peterman, Wuite and Heller 2024 (measurement modes and assays). The full references are in the taxonomy citation file.
